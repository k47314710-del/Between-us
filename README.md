# Our Connected World

the application name is between us and the vision is clear, I’d design the whole thing as a “two phones connected by love” app rather than a conventional couples dashboard.

The key idea:

You select yourself → you can update your own phone.
You select your girlfriend → you enter her shared digital phone and experience her day.

One important design decision

I would not literally copy an iPhone/Android interface. Instead, make it inspired by a phone: a phone-shaped container, status bar, app icons, notifications, lock-screen feel, etc.

That gives you the emotional illusion of:

“I wonder what Varshini's world looks like right now…”

without making the app unnecessarily complicated.
